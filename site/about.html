What We Do

BuyCheapRAM aggregates RAM listings from Newegg and ranks them by price per GB, so you can find the best memory deals without visiting multiple retailer pages. We cover desktop DIMM, laptop SO-DIMM, server RDIMM/UDIMM, and ECC memory across DDR5, DDR4, and DDR3.

Every listing shows the price, capacity, speed, DDR type, form factor, ECC status, and CAS latency — all sortable and filterable. Whether you're building a gaming PC, upgrading a laptop, or provisioning a server, we help you find the best value RAM fast.

Why We Built This

Comparing RAM prices is harder than it should be. Retailer sites show you what's in stock, but they don't always sort by value. A 32GB kit at $60 and a 32GB kit at $120 look similar until you calculate the price per GB. BuyCheapRAM does that math for every single listing, so the best deals always rise to the top.

With RAM prices fluctuating due to global supply conditions, finding good deals matters more than ever. We built this tool to make that process simple and transparent.

How It Works

1. We Collect Data

We regularly scrape Newegg's full RAM catalog, capturing product names, prices, specs, and availability.

2. We Calculate $/GB

Every listing gets a price-per-GB calculation, the single best metric for comparing RAM value across different capacities.

3. You Filter & Sort

Filter by DDR type, form factor, ECC, speed, and capacity. Sort by $/GB, price, or speed to find exactly what you need.

4. You Buy Direct

Click through to the retailer to complete your purchase. We never handle transactions — we just show you where the deals are.
