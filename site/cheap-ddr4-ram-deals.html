💡 Tip: Check if your motherboard supports XMP/DOCP profiles. Without enabling XMP in BIOS, your DDR4 will run at the base 2133/2400MHz speed — wasting the performance you paid for.

DDR4 Speed: Does It Matter?

For most users, DDR4 3200MHz CL16 is the sweet spot. Going from 3200 to 3600MHz gives you 2-5% more FPS in games — noticeable only if you're CPU-bound. The real gains come from capacity: going from 8GB to 16GB (or 16GB to 32GB) makes a much bigger difference than faster MHz.

FAQ

Is DDR4 still good for gaming in 2026?

Yes. DDR4 3200-3600MHz is still perfectly capable for gaming. You won't notice a difference from DDR5 in most games unless you're running a top-tier GPU at 1080p.

Can I add more DDR4 RAM to my existing kit?

You can, but for best stability, buy a new matched kit rather than adding to your existing one. Mixing RAM sticks — even from the same brand — can cause compatibility issues.

What's the difference between DDR4 3200 and 3600?

About 2-5% in real-world performance. DDR4 3600 CL16 is slightly faster but usually costs $5-10 more. For budget builds, 3200 CL16 is the better value.
