How to Find Cheap RAM

Finding cheap RAM is easy if you know what to look for:

1. Sort by Price Per GB ($/GB)

Never compare RAM by total price alone. A $60 32GB kit ($1.88/GB) is cheaper than a $40 16GB kit ($2.50/GB). Always calculate price per GB — or just use our comparison tool which does it automatically.

2. Skip the RGB Tax

RGB RAM costs $10-20 more than identical non-RGB kits. The memory chips are the same — you're paying for lights. For cheap RAM, always choose the non-RGB version.

3. Buy Last-Gen Speeds

DDR5 5200MHz is significantly cheaper than DDR5 6400MHz, and the real-world difference is 2-5%. Same with DDR4 3200 vs 3600MHz. Buy the "slow" kit — it's still fast enough for everything.

4. Watch for Sales

Amazon Prime Day, Black Friday, and Newegg sales often drop RAM prices 20-30%. But even without sales, DDR4 and DDR5 are at historic lows in 2026.

5. Consider Used Server RAM

Used DDR4 ECC RDIMM on eBay is incredibly cheap — often 50% less than new. For home servers and NAS builds, this is the best value. Just run Memtest86 before deploying.

Cheap RAM Doesn't Mean Bad RAM

There's a misconception that cheap RAM is unreliable. In reality, all major brands (Corsair, Kingston, Crucial, G.Skill, TeamGroup) use the same memory chips from Samsung, SK Hynix, or Micron. The difference between a $50 kit and a $100 kit is speed and branding — not reliability.

⚠️ Avoid: No-name brands on marketplace sites. Stick to Corsair, G.Skill, Kingston, Crucial, TeamGroup, Patriot, orADATA. These brands have lifetime warranties and reliable quality control.

Cheap RAM FAQ

What is the cheapest RAM I can buy?

The absolute cheapest RAM is 8GB DDR4 2666MHz, sometimes available for under $15. For most users, 16GB DDR4 3200MHz ($28-38) is the best cheap RAM upgrade.

Is cheap RAM slower than expensive RAM?

Slightly, but not enough to notice. Cheap DDR4 3200MHz vs expensive DDR4 3600MHz is a 2-5% difference. For gaming at 1440p or 4K, the GPU is the bottleneck.

Can cheap RAM cause problems?

Not if you buy from reputable brands. Cheap RAM from Corsair, Kingston, or Crucial uses the same Samsung, SK Hynix, or Micron chips as expensive kits.

Is it worth buying cheap DDR5?

Yes. Cheap DDR5 5200MHz kits ($65-80 for 32GB) are excellent for new builds. They're faster than most DDR4 and only slightly more expensive than budget DDR4.
