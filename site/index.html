faq

Frequently asked questions.

How are RAM deals ranked?

Listings are sorted by price per GB by default, so high-capacity kits with strong value rise to the top.

Can I find DDR5 and DDR4 deals?

Yes. Filter by DDR type to compare cheap DDR5 RAM, DDR4 RAM, and older memory listings.

Does cheap RAM mean bad RAM?

No. Cheap RAM can be a great buy if the DDR type, capacity, speed, latency, and form factor match your system.

Why are RAM prices so high in 2026?

A global shortage of DRAM chips has driven prices up 3-4x since early 2025. BuyCheapRAM helps you find the best available deals.

Is DDR5 worth the extra cost?

DDR5 offers 50-100% more bandwidth, benefiting content creation and AI workloads. For budget builds, DDR4 still offers excellent value.

What is a good price per GB?

In 2026's shortage market, under $3/GB for DDR4 and under $6/GB for DDR5 is a good deal.

Can I use laptop RAM in a desktop?

No. Laptop RAM (SO-DIMM) and desktop RAM (DIMM) have different physical sizes. BuyCheapRAM labels each listing by form factor.

How often are prices updated?

BuyCheapRAM scrapes Newegg's full RAM catalog regularly. During volatile pricing, check back frequently as deals change daily.

What RAM for gaming in 2026?

32GB DDR5 6000MHz CL30-CL32 offers the best value. On a budget, 16GB DDR4 3200MHz still works well for most games.
